Specifications

System Requirements

Optimized for maximum efficiency and low CPU usage.

Minimum Configuration

  • OS: Windows 10 (Build 19041+) 64-bit or macOS 12 Monterey
  • CPU: Intel Core i3 8th Gen / AMD Ryzen 3 / Apple M1
  • RAM: 4 GB RAM
  • Disk: 600 MB free space
  • Display: 1280 × 720 minimum resolution

Recommended for 4K60 Production

  • OS: Windows 11 (23H2+) or macOS 14 Sonoma / Sequoia
  • CPU: Intel Core i7 12th Gen / AMD Ryzen 7 / Apple M2/M3
  • RAM: 16 GB DDR4/DDR5
  • GPU: NVIDIA RTX 3060+ with NVENC (or Apple Silicon)
  • Storage: Fast NVMe SSD
